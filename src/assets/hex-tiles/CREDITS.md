from **Hex Tiles Fantasy** by **cmartins**.

## What we changed

The original tiles were renamed to semantic feature names and packed into a
sprite atlas for in-app rendering. These adaptations (renamed PNGs + generated
atlas) are **likewise released under CC BY-SA 4.0** (the ShareAlike term applies
to the art and its derivatives, not to the surrounding application code).
